# Originality and limitations

AhaBridge combines a teacher-verified intervention loop with misconception-tagged evidence, practical low-tech activities, text/sketch teach-backs and distinct delayed checks. It does not claim to invent diagnostic teaching, adaptive learning, offline content or paper assessment. Eedi, DIKSHA, Kolibri and Plickers have related capabilities. No global uniqueness claim is justified.

Primary references checked 12 November 2026: [Eedi's misconception diagnostic engine](https://www.eedi.com/diagnostic-engine), [DIKSHA's education infrastructure and content tools](https://diksha.gov.in/about/), [Kolibri's offline-first teaching platform](https://learningequality.org/kolibri/about-kolibri/), and [Plickers' student response cards](https://help.plickers.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009395854-What-is-Plickers). These establish related capabilities; they do not prove a global novelty claim. SDG alignment is framed against the [UN's Goal 4 targets](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal4).

The sample is synthetic, small and educationally authored for demonstration. Heuristic cutoffs are editable defaults, not validated psychometrics. Multiple wrong selections suggest a possible misunderstanding, never a cognitive diagnosis. Seed activity review flags are synthetic teacher fixtures, not an external expert certification.

English is the curriculum default. Primary navigation labels and fraction activities have Hindi and Odia translations; visible English fallback is used elsewhere. Translation quality should be reviewed by the deploying school. No full trilingual curriculum claim is made.

Offline mode is intentionally limited: preopened exercises, explicitly saved approved missions, local drafts and queued text/stroke responses. Login, new attempts, teacher review, and delayed assessments require the server. It is not a complete offline school server. Drafts are visible to users of the browser profile, so shared devices need care and logout wipes local records.

The platform steward currently has aggregate health/audit screens. Organization and elevated-account management use a trusted operator CLI rather than a full invitation-management dashboard. Content collections are capped for this small deployment; there is no large-school pagination/search interface. Diagnostic assignment is one set per classroom, with a single attempt per learner. New sets provide new assessments. There is no automated recommendation reorder interface, speech recognition, QR code generator, audio/video upload, external AI or automatic clinical assessment.

Public use requires owner verification of artwork rights and school authorization/consent/retention policies. The software does not itself determine legal compliance. Render preview resources are not a durable production guarantee. See the deployment guide and acceptance report for actual checks and remaining verification limits.
